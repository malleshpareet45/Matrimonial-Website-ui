Siddharth & Anjali

"From our first conversation to our wedding day, every moment felt destined."

event_available Met Dec 2023 church Married Nov 2024

How We Met

"I had almost given up on finding someone who truly understood my balance of career ambition and family values," Siddharth recalls. "When I saw Anjali's profile on EternalUnion, what struck me wasn't just her photo, but her description of her ideal weekend—spending time with grandparents and reading history books. It mirrored my own life so perfectly."

Anjali adds, "I received his interest request on a Tuesday evening. His profile was detailed, respectful, and honest. We started chatting the next day, and within a week, we knew we had to meet."

The First Meeting

"We met at a small coffee shop in South Bombay. A quick 30-minute meeting turned into a 4-hour conversation about everything from our favorite childhood memories to our future aspirations. It felt like reuniting with an old friend."

The Journey to 'Yes'

Over the next six months, their families met and bonded instantly. "The compatibility scores on EternalUnion were spot on," Siddharth laughs. "Our families shared the same cultural background and values, which made the union seamless."

Siddharth proposed during a sunset walk on Marine Drive, the same place they had their second date. "It was simple, private, and perfect," says Anjali.
