How do I hide my profile from others? expand_more
You can change your profile visibility in Settings > Privacy Control. You can choose to be visible to all members, only premium members, or hide your profile completely.
Can I contact members without a premium plan? expand_more
Free members can send interest requests, but to view contact numbers and send personalized messages/chats, a Premium Membership is required.
Is my phone number visible to everyone? expand_more
No, your phone number is private by default. You can control who sees it in Settings > Privacy Control. Most users set it to "Visible on Request".
How do I delete my account? expand_more
To delete your account permanently, please go to Settings > Account Settings and scroll to the bottom to find the "Delete Account" option. Note that this action is irreversible.
